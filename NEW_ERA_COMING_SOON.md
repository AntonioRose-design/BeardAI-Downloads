## A radically upgraded BeardAI experience

BeardAI's next direction brings a more sophisticated command-style interface, clearer evidence-based game planning, better local data protection, smarter workflow navigation, and carefully reviewed community features.

The private engineering work currently includes development prototypes of:

- **Sovereign Command:** an integrated game-observation and personal priority dashboard.
- **Sovereign Search:** quickly jump to available workspace tools.
- **Evidence Lab:** review user-entered game readings, screenshots and history; do not confuse preview with automatic image recognition.
- **Decision Studio & Scenario Lab:** compare plans, hypothetical resource costs and milestones based on values you provide.
- **Sovereign Vault:** move selected workspace records using local, unencrypted JSON backups.
- **Device Readiness Center:** surface local feature support and platform limitations.

These are undergoing further development and testing; they are **not included in the current public build**.
